# The game's files

A project made for the game also holds the game's files as they are on its discs: the **Disc**, its **Stages**,
**Folders**, **File groups** and **Files**, **Resource bundles**, **Movie files**, the **Executable**, **Scene audio**
and plain **Text** and **Binary data**.

They come from filling the project from your discs, and are what the other assets are read from. They have no
views of their own: open one with **Files** (`Ctrl+2`) to see its contents as text or bytes.
