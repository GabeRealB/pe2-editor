# Movies

The game's movies are **Movie** assets. They play in the player, with their sound, and show a frame from a quarter
of the way in as their picture.

## Replacing a movie

- **Movie, from a video file** makes a movie from an MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM or AVI file.
- With a movie selected, importing a video file replaces that movie's source.

## Exporting

**File ▸ Export…** saves a movie as a video file. This needs the `pe2.ffmpeg` extension, which ships beside the
editor; see its own guide in the **Extensions** pane.
